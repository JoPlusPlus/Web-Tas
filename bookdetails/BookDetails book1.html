The pillars of the earth,

Written by ken follett

About the Book

The story centers on the ambitious, decades-long construction of a magnificent Gothic cathedral in the fictional town of Kingsbridge. Through this monumental project, the lives of a devoted prior (Philip), a visionary master builder (Tom Builder), an ingenious outcast (Jack), and a resilient noblewoman (Aliena) intertwine. Together, they battle ruthless rivals, corrupt clergy, and political intrigue to bring their masterpiece to life.
